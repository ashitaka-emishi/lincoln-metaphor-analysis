# V4 Corpus Expansion Impact Report

Generated from the v1 manifest-derived inventory, v4 core inventory, v4 validation inventory, and v4 coverage summary.

## What Changed

The v4 core expands the baseline from 28 to 48 core documents and adds a 78-document validation corpus.

The v4 core preserves the 28-document v1 baseline and adds 20 core documents. The project also now tracks an extended validation corpus for denominator and coverage checks.

## What Did Not Change

V4 reduces but does not eliminate selection-bias risk. Existing interpretive claims remain limited to the currently annotated core until the v4 additions are fully re-analyzed.

The validation corpus is lightly annotated and does not replace the fully annotated core as evidence for coded metaphor findings.

## Claims Strengthened

- Slavery and emancipation: 20 core records and 36 validation records now match this area.
- Race and Black military agency: 2 core records and 4 validation records now match this area.
- Register and genre breadth: The core expansion adds private letters, public letters, annual messages, proclamations, fragments, condolences, and campaign/public speeches.

## Claims Still Limited

- Currently coded interpretive findings: Until the v4 additions are fully re-annotated, interpretive claims remain limited to the currently annotated core evidence.
- Disease/purification negative finding: 0 core records match the metadata-level negative-check proxy, so this absence remains a re-testing target rather than a settled corpus-expansion result.

## Claims Requiring Re-Testing

- Metaphor frequency and distribution claims: The denominator changes from 28 v1 documents to a 48-document v4 core, so counts and proportions must be regenerated before publication claims are updated.
- War powers: 2 core records and 13 validation records indicate useful coverage, but the coverage summary still marks this area as caution/follow-up.
- Providence and sacrifice: The added condolence, fragment, and late-war materials broaden the evidence base, but Stage 4A coding is needed before interpretive language changes.

## Claims Not Supported by Corpus Expansion Alone

- Claims based only on validation or reference records: The extended validation corpus is lightly annotated, and reference records are search-only leads.
- Claims about all Lincoln writings: V4 improves coverage but remains a curated research corpus, not a complete population of Lincoln texts.
- Revised absence claims: Selection expansion can expose risk, but absence findings require full annotation and concordance-backed re-analysis.

## Limitations

- V4 reduces but does not eliminate selection-bias risk.
- The v4 core is the full-annotation target; new v4 core additions still require Stage 4A re-analysis before they can revise coded interpretive claims.
- The validation corpus is lightly annotated and supports coverage checks, denominator checks, and source-discovery review rather than direct metaphor-count claims.
- Reference records remain search-only leads and cannot support claims without later ingestion, segmentation, annotation, and validation.
